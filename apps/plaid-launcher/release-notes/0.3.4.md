## What's new

- **Much smaller installer and updates.** The installer is about 3 MB. Plaid downloads its recompiler (about 300 MB) once on first start and checks it before use; app-only updates stay small.
- **Controllers work out of the box.** A plugged-in controller drives Player 1 alongside the keyboard, without changing any setting.
- **Smoother audio.** Short hitches no longer tear the sound or skip a fifth of a second.
- **Paper Mario builds again.** Builds use the full analysis by default, which the newer automatic repair step had broken for some games.
- **Honest settings.** The Experimental tab is gone; its switches were saved but never used by games. The aspect ratio setting controls widescreen.
- **Publisher shows as sting-raider** in Windows' installed apps list.

## Known limitations

- The first start needs an internet connection to download the recompiler; Compile waits until it finishes. Recompile your games once after updating.
- Paper Mario crashes after choosing a save file, the Rogue Squadron intro stutters back and forth, F-Zero X slows down in some menus, and some games show graphics glitches. These are being worked on.
- The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may warn before running it.
