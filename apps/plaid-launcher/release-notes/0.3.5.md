## What's new

- **The right game info.** The About panel shows each game's own Nintendo 64 article; Ocarina of Time and Majora's Mask showed the 2026 remake before.
- **Clearer recompiler download.** The button fills as it downloads and says when it is unpacking, instead of stopping at 100%.
- **Smoother update button.** The download ring and its arrow draw correctly, the notes say what is happening, "Installing…" shows while the update installs, and the notes stay open long enough to scroll.

## Known limitations

- Paper Mario crashes after choosing a save file, the Rogue Squadron intro stutters back and forth, F-Zero X slows down in some menus, and some games show graphics glitches. These are being worked on.
- The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may warn before running it.
